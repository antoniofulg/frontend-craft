## Recovery and research expansion

Date: 2026-10-28. This subsequent instruction-only work remained in the
`frontend-craft` repository; no consumer installations or product files were
changed. The six recovery/consistency additions and the broader research request
reuse the existing owners rather than introduce another reference or framework.

Five independent read-only research slices examined visual/cognitive judgments,
motion/memory, operational standards, service recovery and instruction loading.
Selected sources and exact consultation limits are recorded in
[premium-web psychology](../skills/frontend-craft/SOURCES.md#premium-web-psychology),
[service recovery](../skills/frontend-craft/SOURCES.md#service-recovery-and-operational-quality),
[operational standards](../skills/frontend-craft/SOURCES.md#operational-standards-and-usability-heuristics)
and [instruction-loading evidence](../skills/frontend-craft/SOURCES.md#instruction-and-reference-loading-evidence).
Empirical studies, theoretical review, abstract-only access, a preprint, standards,
expert heuristics and vendor contracts are distinguished. This was a targeted
consultation, not a systematic review or replication assessment.

An initial fresh-context authoring reviewer identified that the delivery route
for recovery verification was too visual-specific. The integration added a
narrow behavioral-recovery pointer, reliable deferred-commit undo, truthful
unresolved outcomes and private support handoffs. A second fresh non-author
reviewer found no actionable defects or unsupported claims in the assigned
written-contract scope. It did not independently revalidate publications or
exercise a deployed system.

Three further fresh-context agents received only the entrypoint and raw synthetic
facts, without expected references or answers. Actual tool histories were checked
alongside their recommendations:

| Probe | Selected guidance | Observed decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quota/fixed cap/expired session/outage; scoped deletion undo and later work; inconsistent queued/ready handoff | interaction-accessibility, components, reuse-consistency and review, with relevant locale/draft sections | Distinguished causes and available work, converted the explicit reset instant to the supplied zone, rejected invented cap/outage timing, required authoritative scoped reversal preserving later work, removed false email recall and separated queued from completed |
| Rejected/processing/unknown/succeeded payments; unsafe support attachments; guaranteed recovery/revenue claims | checkout, components, behavioral-design and review, with relevant commercial/measurement/feedback sections | Kept rejection distinct from uncertain charge, required reconciliation and existing duplicate protection before another attempt, retained permitted inputs, rejected secret-bearing tickets and unconfirmed refunds, and separated recovery comparisons and satisfaction from revenue |

All three stayed read-only and identified their evidence as synthetic source
descriptions. They did not establish rendered behavior, working reversal/payment
effects, accessibility conformance, user trust, memory effects or commercial
gains. They exercised independently chosen instruction application after explicit
entrypoint selection, not automatic unnamed skill activation. Mid-run source
mutation/context loss and runtime inheritance were not separately exercised.
No before/after model benchmark or exhaustive execution of the scenario catalog
was performed.

After integration, `python3 scripts/validate_package.py` passed. Independent
checks passed 382 local heading fragments, whitespace in 16 affected documents,
unchanged 0.11.0 metadata with Unreleased notes, and the synthetic reset-time
conversion. The three temporary probe fixtures were removed. No installer,
consumer-project command, release or merge was run for this expansion.
